# awaaz-legal

Public legal documents for **Awaaz**, a voice-only social network for Android
(`com.awaaz.app`).

They are hosted here so their version history is public and auditable — when a
policy changes, the change is in the git log rather than in a silent edit.

| Document | What it covers | Referenced by |
|---|---|---|
| [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) | What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights. Includes the **Grievance Officer** details required by India's IT Rules 2021. | Play Store listing · in-app Settings |
| [Terms of Service](terms.md) | The rules of the platform, what you may post, and how moderation and appeals work. | In-app, accepted before your first post |
| [Child Safety Standards](child-safety.md) | Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), and how reports are handled. | Play Console CSAE declaration |
| [Account Deletion](delete-account.md) | How to delete your account, in-app or by request, and exactly what is deleted or retained. | Play Store data-deletion URL |
